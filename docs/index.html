Quick answers about offline AI subtitles, pricing, compatibility, and features.

Is VOXORA Video Player really 100% free?
Yes. VOXORA is 100% free to download and use with no subscriptions, no locked pro tiers, and full access to VOXORA Smart Subtitles (Offline AI Engine), Dual Subtitles, 200% Volume Booster, Dedicated Music Player, and the Private Biometric Vault.
Does VOXORA work completely offline?
Yes. Local video playback, the Music Player, Equalizer, Lossless Clip Cutter, and Private Vault all work 100% offline. After a quick one-time language pack download inside the app, VOXORA Smart Subtitles (Offline AI Engine) runs directly on your device even in airplane mode.
How do I enable auto AI subtitles in VOXORA?
Play any video, tap the CC pill in the top-right player bar, select your preferred language from 59+ supported languages, and enable Auto Subtitles. Subtitles begin appearing within seconds anchored to your exact playback timestamp—even when seeking or resuming mid-movie.
Can I display Dual Subtitles or export .SRT subtitle files?
Yes. You can toggle Dual Subtitles to view original dialogue and your translated language together—ideal for learning new languages—and export generated speech-synced subtitles as standard .SRT files directly to your storage.
Which Android versions and devices are supported?
VOXORA supports 64-bit Android smartphones and tablets running Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer with an arm64-v8a processor, which covers virtually all modern Android devices.
When is AI Audio Dubbing coming to VOXORA?
AI Audio Dubbing is currently on our active roadmap and marked as Coming Soon. When it launches, VOXORA's built-in GitHub update checker will notify you automatically so you can upgrade in one tap.
